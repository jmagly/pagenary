# Launch readiness checklist

Use this quick list to verify Atlas Edge is production-ready before your first autonomous run.

- Create tenant users with role-based access and hardware pairing rights.
- Register facilities and map relay IDs to local power domains.
- Validate low-latency telemetry streaming from at least one test rover.
- Confirm incident response policies and escalation contacts are configured.

## Day-of launch timeline

1. **Briefing** — Align operations leads on the change window and observability dashboards.
2. **Activation** — Enable command relays and confirm dual uplinks are active.
3. **Smoke test** — Execute the “hello relay” task from the Atlas CLI and review logs.
4. **Sign-off** — Capture acceptance criteria across the safety, reliability, and data teams.
