# Safety

An interocitor is a focused-energy device with a live aperture. Treat it with the
respect you'd give any array that can reach somewhere you can't see.

## Interlocks

- **Containment ring.** No power flows until the ring reports closed. Do not
  defeat it.
- **Exclusion zone.** Keep the focal volume clear during a sweep. The aperture is
  small, but what comes through it may not be.
- **Reference loss.** If the oscillator drops lock, the array safes itself and
  collapses the aperture. Let it.

## The prime directive

If something answers on your band that you did not hail, **log it and disengage.**
Do not relay a payload to an unidentified correspondent. The Labs maintain a
contact registry for a reason; use it.

> Curiosity is an excellent engineering trait and a poor first-contact protocol.

## In an emergency

Cut the reference oscillator. Everything downstream depends on it, so killing the
phase lock is the fastest way to bring the whole array to a safe, dark stop.
