# The Interocitor

An **interocitor** is a modular array for communication and matter-relay across
distances that make conventional radio look quaint. Interocitor Labs designs the
arrays, ships them as component kits, and helps you stand one up without losing a
technician to the focal triad.

## What the array does

- **Reaches.** A calibrated interocitor maintains a stable channel across the
  Metaluna band with sub-second latency.
- **Relays.** Beyond voice and telemetry, a tuned array can relay small,
  inert payloads through the same aperture.
- **Listens.** The triad is bidirectional. If something is broadcasting on your
  band, you will hear it.

## Who builds one

Research stations, long-haul logistics, and anyone whose nearest neighbor is
several light-minutes away. If a courier drone is your current comms strategy,
an interocitor pays for itself on the first storm season.
