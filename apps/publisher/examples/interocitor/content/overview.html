What the array does

  • Reaches. A calibrated interocitor holds a stable channel across the Metaluna band with sub-second latency.
  • Relays. Beyond voice and telemetry, a tuned array can relay small, inert payloads through the same aperture.
  • Listens. The triad is bidirectional. If something is broadcasting on your band, you will hear it.

Who builds one

Research stations, long-haul logistics, and anyone whose nearest neighbor is several light-minutes away. If a courier drone is your current comms strategy, an interocitor pays for itself on the first storm season.
